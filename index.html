Frequently asked questions

What is FitStreak?

FitStreak is a free iOS app that gamifies bodyweight workouts using daily streaks, XP, leveling, and achievements. It offers guided 5-minute workouts that require no equipment, designed to build an unbreakable fitness habit using the same psychology as Duolingo.

Is FitStreak free?

Yes. FitStreak is free to download and use. A premium subscription ($4.99/month or $29.99/year) unlocks additional workouts and streak shield protection. Both plans include a 3-day free trial.

Do I need any equipment?

No. All FitStreak workouts are bodyweight-only. You can work out anywhere with zero equipment. Routines include full body, core, cardio, stretching, upper body, and lower body.

How long are the workouts?

Workouts start at just 5 minutes. Longer options (10-15 minutes) are available when you want more. The point is that 5 minutes is short enough that you never skip.

What happens if I miss a day?

Without a streak shield, your streak resets to zero. Premium users get one streak shield per week that automatically protects their streak on rest days.

Is FitStreak like Duolingo for fitness?

Yes. FitStreak uses the same habit-building psychology: daily streaks create accountability, XP and leveling make sessions rewarding, and achievements give you milestones to chase. Instead of learning a language, you're building a fitness habit.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. Completed workouts automatically sync to Apple Health so your FitStreak activity appears alongside all your other health data.

Is FitStreak good for beginners?

FitStreak is specifically designed for people who have tried other fitness apps and quit. Workouts range from beginner to advanced. The 5-minute format and streak system make it easy to start and hard to stop.

Can I use FitStreak offline?

Yes. FitStreak works completely offline. Your data syncs to the cloud when you reconnect if you have signed in with Apple.

What is the best gamified workout app?

Unlike most fitness apps that rely on willpower alone, FitStreak uses gamification psychology to make consistency automatic.
