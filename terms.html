4. Health and safety

FitStreak is not a medical device and does not give medical, physiotherapy or nutrition advice. It does not replace a doctor or other qualified professional, and it cannot know your health.

Check with a doctor before you start, or before you make your training harder, if any of these apply to you:

  • you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, or have ever had chest pain, fainting or dizzy spells
  • you have a joint, bone or back problem, an injury, or have had recent surgery
  • you are pregnant or have recently given birth
  • you have diabetes, asthma or another long-term condition, or take medicine that affects your heart rate, blood pressure or balance
  • you have not exercised for a long time, or you are unsure whether exercise is safe for you

Stop straight away if you feel pain, chest pain or pressure, severe shortness of breath, dizziness or faintness, or an irregular heartbeat. If it does not pass quickly, get medical help. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Choose exercises that suit you and train in a safe space. If an exercise uses a chair, wall or other furniture, make sure it is sturdy and will not slide. The movement limits setting helps filter exercises, but it cannot guarantee that every exercise is right for your body. Skip anything that does not feel right.

Streaks are there to encourage you, not to push you through pain or illness. Rest when you need to; rest days and streak protection exist for that.

Calorie, nutrition and intensity numbers are estimates and can be wrong. The food and calorie features are not designed to manage a medical condition or an eating disorder. If you are struggling with eating, weight or body image, please talk to a doctor or a local eating disorder support service.

5. AI features

AI workouts, programs and food photo estimates are generated by a third-party AI model (Google Gemini) from what you enter. AI output can be wrong, incomplete or unsuitable for you. Check every exercise and number yourself before relying on it, and skip anything that does not suit you. AI output is not medical or nutrition advice. Please do not enter information about health conditions or other sensitive details in AI requests.

AI features have monthly limits, which the app shows. Limits can change.
