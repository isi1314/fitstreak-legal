# FitStreak: the gym pet that texts you about your streak

A workout streak app for people who have started and stopped before. A companion that grows with every workout, 5-minute workouts, and chair routines. Free on iPhone.

## It sounds like this

Reminders come from your companion, in its own voice. These are real lines from the app.

> Foxfire: Foxfire is here when you’re ready. A short session today keeps your 12-day streak going, if it fits.

> Sprout: Sprout is stretching toward today.

> Foxfire: Foxfire is secretly thrilled. Day 13!

Miss a day and your companion doesn't scold you: "Sprout says a streak can start again whenever you feel ready."

## How it works

1. Answer a few questions and meet the companion that matches you.
2. Do a short workout today, at home, at your desk or at the gym.
3. Keep your streak and watch your companion grow through 5 stages.

## Made for real beginners

- **Five minutes counts.** Workouts start at 5 minutes and every one counts toward your streak.
- **Nothing jumps, if you'd rather not.** 10 low-impact routines are done seated or holding a chair. 6 of them are free.
- **Tell it what to avoid.** Flag movements like jumping or getting down on the floor, and workouts pushed at you leave them out.
- **Rest without losing it.** Turn on Rest Days, so a day off after a good week doesn't cost you the streak.

## Free and Premium

Free: your companion, the streak, 22 workouts including 6 chair routines, Rest Days, reminders in your companion's voice and the home screen widget.

Premium adds all 70 workouts, a weekly streak freeze and planned days off, seasonal outfits for your companion, and up to 60 AI workouts or programs and 120 food scans a month (free accounts get 3 of each to try). In the US it costs $24.99 a year, $9.99 a month or $6.99 a week, or $79.99 once.
